QBank support

QBank is an iPhone and iPad app for practicing questions on any course or topic: you answer single-answer, select-all, ordering, matching, matrix, highlight and drop-down questions, read the rationale, and come back to what you missed.

Get help

Email dimitrygallego@gmail.com. Please say which iPhone or iPad and which iOS version you use, and what you did before the problem appeared. Never send your password. Replies are best effort.

If your question is about importing a file of questions, the question file format lists what a file may hold. The importer names the question and the field it refused, so the message is usually the fastest way to the fix.

Delete your account

In the app, open Settings > Delete account and enter your password. The server deletes the account for good, with everything linked to it: your study content, answers, progress and sign-in. If you want a copy first, use Export my progress in Settings.

If you cannot sign in, or cannot reach that screen, email dimitrygallego@gmail.com from the address of your account and ask for the account to be deleted. The privacy policy lists exactly what is deleted and how long backups keep a copy.
